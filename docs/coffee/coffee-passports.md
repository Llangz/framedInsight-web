# Coffee Digital Passports

Version: 1.0

---

# 1. Business Purpose

Coffee Passports transform FramedInsight from a farm management
platform into a traceability and transparency platform.

The objective is to allow buyers and consumers to verify:

- Origin
- Sustainability
- Quality
- Cooperative identity
- Export details
